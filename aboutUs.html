About AMELTAN Akwa Ibom

The Association of Medical Laboratory Technician and Assistant Nigeria (AMELTAN) – Akwa Ibom State Chapter is a professional body committed to the advancement of medical laboratory practice. We serve as a voice for technicians and assistants, promoting ethical standards, continuous education, and collaboration across healthcare sectors.

Our members play a vital role in diagnostic services, ensuring accuracy and reliability in laboratory results. Through workshops, seminars, and advocacy, we empower our professionals to stay current with global best practices.

AMELTAN Akwa Ibom is dedicated to fostering unity, professional growth, and recognition for all medical laboratory technicians and assistants in the state.
