Our Services

  • Professional Development: Workshops, seminars, and training programs to enhance technical skills and knowledge.
  • Certification & Registration: Guidance and support for members seeking official recognition and documentation.
  • Advocacy: Representing the interests of technicians and assistants in healthcare policy and regulation.
  • Networking Opportunities: Events and forums to connect with peers, mentors, and industry leaders.
  • Ethical Standards: Promoting best practices and professional ethics in laboratory operations.
  • Community Outreach: Health awareness campaigns and volunteer initiatives across Akwa Ibom State.
